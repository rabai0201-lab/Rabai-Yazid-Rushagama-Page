PERSONAL INFORMATION

About Rabai Yazid Rushagama

My name is Rabai Yazid Rushagama , a Tanzanian student and technology enthusiast with an academic background that combines Accountancy and Information Technology . I am currently pursuing a Bachelor's Degree in Accountancy with Information Technology at the Institute of Accountancy Arusha (IAA).

My academic journey has given me an opportunity to develop knowledge in both the financial and technological sides of modern organizations. I am interested in understanding how accounting, business, information systems and technology can work together to solve practical problems.

My interests, however, extend beyond the classroom. I have a strong interest in web development, programming, databases, networking and artificial intelligence . I enjoy learning how digital systems are built and how technology can be used to create useful solutions for individuals, organizations and communities.

I also consider myself a creative person. Writing and storytelling are important parts of my personal development. I enjoy writing books, developing stories, creating characters, exploring scripts and developing creative concepts . This creative side allows me to approach problems and ideas from different perspectives.

One of my biggest interests is the combination of technology and creativity . I am particularly interested in how artificial intelligence, software and digital media can transform an idea into something people can use, watch, read or experience.

Throughout my education and practical experience, I have had opportunities to work in both accounting and information technology environments. These experiences have helped me develop professional discipline, communication skills, problem-solving ability and an understanding of workplace responsibilities.

I believe that continuous learning is essential in a world where technology and professional requirements are constantly changing. For this reason, I continue to explore new technologies, improve my technical abilities and develop my understanding of business and finance.

My long-term career interests include accountancy, information technology, web development, software development, database administration and networking . At the same time, I want to continue developing myself as a writer, storyteller and creative technology enthusiast.

My broader ambition is to build a career that allows me to combine professional knowledge, technological skills and creativity to develop meaningful solutions and projects with an impact beyond my immediate environment.
